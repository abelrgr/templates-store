Our Restoration Process

From discovery to delivery, here's how we bring furniture back to life

1. Collect

Phase One

We scour estate sales, auctions, and private donations to find hidden gems with potential. Each piece is carefully selected for its craftsmanship, era, and restoration possibilities.

2. Restore

Phase Two

Our master craftsmen assess, repair, and refinish each piece using traditional techniques. We strip old finishes, repair structural damage, replace worn components, and apply new stains or paints.

3. Style

Phase Three

Professional staging brings out each piece's character. We add new hardware, reupholster with premium fabrics, and style accessories to showcase the furniture's full potential.

4. Sell

Phase Four

Ready for its second life! Each restored piece is photographed, listed, and delivered to its new home with care instructions and a satisfaction guarantee.
